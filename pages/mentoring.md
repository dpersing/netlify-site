# Mentoring

Are you looking to get into the digital accessibility field, or need support in navigating it? I offer personalized mentoring services that can help.

## Getting to know you

To get started, I ask that you set up a [1-hour Zoom call](https://calendar.app.google/o7wVZ7g6VdC6Eng36) so we can connect and make sure I'm a good fit for what you're looking for in a mentor. This is your opportunity to tell me about yourself and what you're seeking in mentorship.

## Mentoring session

Mentoring sessions are 1 hour over Zoom to discuss your dreams, challenges, and needs around getting into or doing digital accessibility work. We'll collaborate to find strategies, resources, and reasonable, measurable goals that work for you so you feel like you're making progress. We can meet as few or as many times as you need.

**A 60 USD fee per session is requested but not required for this service.** Payment can be made before or after the session to [@a11ydevon on Venmo](https://account.venmo.com/u/a11ydevon) or through [Wise](https://wise.com) outside of the US. If you're able to expense or write off mentoring, I'm also happy to provide an invoice!

**Payment is absolutely not required nor expected if you are experiencing financial hardship for any reason.** These reasons include but are not limited to: disability, unemployment, immigrant or refugee status, being unhoused, or systemic oppression because of your race, ethnicity, gender, sexuality, geography, religion or spiritual beliefs, and so on.

## Further questions

Have more questions about these sessions, their format, or need an accommodation I didn't include here? [Contact me](/contact/index.html) through this site or email me directly at **devon at dpersing dot com** to let me know, and we'll make it work.
